Pull up a map. Find *South America* on the left and **Africa** on the right. ~~There is no shortcut.~~ The journey is `sea-only` and [begins in Cape Town](https://www.tristandc.com/)[^distance].

## A Rock at the Edge of the World

The island has no airport and can only be reached by sea.

![Map locating Tristan da Cunha in the South Atlantic](articles/tristan-da-cunha/map-v1.svg "Tristan da Cunha lies far from inhabited land.")

- No airport
- A journey measured in days

1. Check the weather
2. Board the ship

> The sea is the only road home.
> — A Tristan resident

---

:::fact{title="No runway, no shortcut"}
The journey from Cape Town takes several days by ship.
:::

:::note{title="A small community"}
The settlement is a living place, not a museum.
:::

:::warning{title="Weather decides"}
Sea conditions can delay every arrival and departure.
:::

[^distance]: The nearest inhabited land is roughly 2,400 km away.
